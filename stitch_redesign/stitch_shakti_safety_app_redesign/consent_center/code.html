Consent Center

Control telemetry collection, sensor permissions, and emergency broadcast retention. Every permission can be changed at any time.

Background Operation Limitation

Notice: Shake and voice triggers work only while the app is open.

Operating system privacy constraints suspend motion sensors and live microphone loops once the display locks or the app is minimized.

Live Location Tracking

What: High accuracy GPS coordinates and nearby cell tower telemetry are collected.

When: Captured strictly during active SOS dispatch or scheduled Safe Journey walks.

Retention: Location samples are permanently deleted after 24 hours unless marked for legal review.

Live Audio Recording

What: Ambient microphone audio is recorded in rolling 15 second encrypted segments.

When: Begins automatically upon SOS activation or loud alarm trigger.

Retention: Audio packets are purged 48 hours after incident resolution.

Shake to Trigger

What: Accelerometer data monitors rapid three-shake physical distress gestures.

When: Evaluated in real time exclusively while the SHAKTI app is open in foreground.

Retention: Sensor motion samples are never stored or transmitted off this device.

Voice Keyword Trigger

What: On-device speech recognition monitors for the emergency distress phrase.

When: Processed continuously on device hardware only while the app is active on screen.

Retention: Voice audio is processed locally and discarded immediately without logging.

Emergency Circle Broadcast

What: Transmits coordinates, battery percentage, and audio web link to your 3 circle contacts.

When: Dispatched immediately via data packet or carrier SMS when SOS is initiated.

Retention: Dispatch logs are stored for 7 days in your local incident history.
