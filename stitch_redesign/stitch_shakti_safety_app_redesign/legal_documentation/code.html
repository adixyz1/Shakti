Legal & Limitations

Plain-language operational policies, data covenants, and statutory boundaries.

Statutory Emergency Notice

SHAKTI is a community alert and peer-tracking tool. It does not replace 112 (Police, Fire, Medical) or 181 (Women Helpline).

Statutory Scope & Limitations

Doc 01 / Ref 2024-A

SHAKTI operates as an unassisted peer-to-peer telemetry protocol. The platform activates rapid signals across personal trusted circles and volunteer networks within immediate proximity.

Foreground Execution Boundary

Due to operating system battery and security constraints, shake detection and voice keyword triggers function reliably only while the screen is unlocked or the application is held in the active foreground.

Cellular & Hardware Range

Packet transmission requires carrier telemetry or cellular data. When out of signal range, requests queue locally in device flash memory until standard GSM or Wi-Fi connectivity re-establishes.

System Capabilities & Explicit Constraints
What SHAKTI DOES
  • Local on-device AES-256 encryption of all telemetry before transit.
  • 1-touch escalation sending live coordinates directly to 5 preselected contacts.
  • Standard offline SMS fallback using unformatted cell tower text pipelines.
  • Zero analytics trackers, zero behavioral logs, zero advertising frameworks.
What SHAKTI DOES NOT DO
  • Does not dispatch armed state police officers or emergency responders without manual routing to 112.
  • Does not record ambient background audio without an intentional trigger event.
  • Does not sell, broker, or syndicate device telemetry to commercial aggregators.
  • Does not guarantee transmission when cellular radios are unpowered or disconnected.

Open Source Utility Terms

Doc 03 / MIT Utility

SHAKTI is distributed under permissive public utility licensing. You preserve complete autonomy over the distribution of your telemetry signals and trusted contact listings.

User-Controlled Dispatch Covenant

The user explicitly authorizes automated outbound SMS transmission and peer notification upon local trigger actuation. No central gatekeeper or authority mediates, delays, or filters this dispatch.

Operational Resiliency & Disclaimers

The software is provided without warranty regarding continuous uninterrupted carrier connectivity or battery preservation during persistent incident broadcasting.

Statutory Escalation Obligation

In any scenario posing imminent bodily harm or acute danger, users must trigger sovereign emergency hotlines (112 or 181) as the binding statutory tier of civil protection.
